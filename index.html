Who We Are

Clean Water is Not a Luxury — It's a Right

Shri Vinayak R.O. Sales & Service has been serving the people of Alwar with top-quality water purification systems. We believe every household deserves access to safe, pure drinking water.

From installation to annual maintenance, we're with you at every step — offering honest pricing, genuine spare parts, and fast on-site support.

Services

Everything Your Water Needs

01

Installation

Professional installation of RO purifiers with zero-mess service. We handle everything — from plumbing to first flush testing.

02

Annual Maintenance

Comprehensive AMC plans with filter replacement, membrane cleaning, and performance checks — keeping your water pure year-round.

03

Filter Replacement

Genuine OEM filters and membranes replaced by trained technicians. We test TDS levels before and after every service.

04

Repair & Troubleshooting

Fast response to low-pressure issues, leaks, unusual taste complaints, or UV failure. Most repairs resolved in a single visit.

05

Water Quality Testing

On-site TDS and pH testing with a detailed report. Know exactly what's in your water before and after purification.

06

Commercial Solutions

High-capacity RO systems for offices, restaurants, and factories. Custom designed for your flow-rate and usage requirements.
